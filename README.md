## 🎯 Core Capabilities: The Five Pillars of Dominance

### ♾️ Vitality Anchor (Infinite Health Protocol)
The Vitality Anchor operates on a sophisticated memory-scanning algorithm that continuously monitors your hunter's HP pool. When incoming damage would drop you below critical thresholds, the anchor triggers a reallocation of internal resources—effectively maintaining your health curve at optimal combat levels. This isn't crude invincibility; it's *smart survivability* that keeps the challenge intact while removing the frustration of one-shot mechanics.

**Benefits:**
- Survive multi-hit combos from Arch-Tempered elders
- Maintain aggressive positioning without retreating to heal
- Perfect for speed-running techniques that require constant pressure

### 🪙 Zenny Reservoir (Resource Multiplier Engine)
The golden tooth of the monster economy—Zenny—flows like a river with the Reservoir active. This feature doesn't fabricate currency from nothing; instead, it intercepts the game's reward-calculation module and applies a scalable multiplier (configurable from 2x to 10x) to every completed quest, captured endemic life, and sold craft material.

**Configuration Presets:**
- **Balanced (2x):** Preserves the feeling of progression
- **Aggressive (5x):** For those building multiple armor sets
- **Megalomaniac (10x):** When you want every weapon tree unlocked in a week

### ⚔️ One-Hit Verdict (Critical Resolution System)
This pillar embodies the hunter's philosophy: *every opening is a potential finisher*. The Critical Resolution System analyzes hit-registration events in real-time, amplifying your strike's damage calculation when the target's posture is broken, exhausted, or trapped. The result? A single well-placed True Charged Slash or Great Sword tackle sends even the mightiest monster to the carving screen.

**Notable Interactions:**
- Works with all 14 weapon types
- Respects monster-specific hit zones
- Compatible with status effects (paralysis, sleep, blast)

### ⚡ Tempo Override (Speed & Timer Manipulation)
Time is the one resource no hunter can craft—until now. The Tempo Override module provides two distinct modes:

- **Chrono Acceleration:** Slows in-game timer progression while maintaining combat fluidity. Perfect for extended investigation windows or capturing rarer species.
- **Iterative Compression:** Speeds up travel animations, gathering sequences, and item-use frames (up to 1.5x) without affecting monster behavior—meaning you'll never miss an attack window due to slow potion-drinking.

### 📦 Inventory Allocator (Item Quantity Multiplier)
Say goodbye to returning to camp for more Mega Potions. The Inventory Allocator intercepts item-gain events from gathering nodes, monster carves, and quest rewards, multiplying stack sizes by a user-defined factor (2x–99x). This turns every herb patch into a pharmaceutical factory and every mining outcrop into a jewel emporium.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: The Arsenal fails to load when I start the game.**
A: Ensure you've launched the game first, then the Arsenal. Verify Windows Defender isn't quarantining the DLL files—add the application folder to your antivirus exclusions list.

**Q: Can I use this while playing with friends online?**
A: Yes, the Arsenal doesn't modify network packets. Your teammates won't see any difference in your character's behavior beyond your increased efficiency. The multiplayer lobby (up to 4 players) works flawlessly.

**Q: Is there a risk of a permaban?**
A: The memory-resident approach and offline-only operation minimize detection vectors significantly. However, no tool is 100% undetectable. Use wisdom—don't join random public lobbies with absurdly high multipliers active.
